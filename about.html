About Hospitality English Academy

Our Commitment to Hospitality Excellence

Hospitality English Academy was founded with a clear and powerful mission: to bridge the language gap that often stands between a talented hospitality professional and a truly world-class guest experience. In an industry where every word matters—from the warmth of a first greeting to the precision of a menu description—communication in English is not merely a skill; it is the foundation of outstanding service.

Since our inception, we have been dedicated to creating content that is authentic, industry-specific, and immediately applicable. Our courses are not built in a classroom; they are built in the dining rooms and lobbies of real hotels and restaurants, in collaboration with industry veterans who understand what excellence truly looks like from the guest's perspective.

Our Story

Hospitality English Academy began as a small initiative by a group of passionate educators who saw a critical gap in professional training. While many programs taught general English, none adequately prepared a restaurant server to describe a tasting menu with confidence, or a hotel concierge to handle a complex VIP request with grace. We set out to change that.

What started as a single module for a boutique hotel group in London has grown into a comprehensive 20-lesson platform used by professionals in over 30 countries. Our journey has been guided by a single belief: that fluency in hospitality English is a career accelerator that opens doors to opportunities in the finest establishments worldwide.

Our Mission

To empower hospitality professionals worldwide with the language skills needed to communicate with confidence, clarity, and warmth. We strive to:

  • Deliver industry-accurate, authentically scripted English training materials
  • Promote a standard of verbal excellence that mirrors the best in the industry
  • Foster a global community of bilingual hospitality professionals
  • Bridge the gap between professional training and real-world guest interaction
  • Inspire a service culture where language is a tool of hospitality, not a barrier

Educational Approach

At Hospitality English Academy, we believe that effective language training must be:

Contextual: Our curriculum is built around real scenarios—a table complaint, a hotel upgrade request, a wine pairing recommendation—ensuring that learners develop language skills they can immediately deploy in their work environment.

Dialogues-Based: Each lesson features an extended, authentic dialogue between a hospitality professional and a guest. This conversational approach ensures that learners develop natural, fluent responses rather than memorized scripts.

Vocabulary-Rich: We introduce and explain industry-specific vocabulary in every lesson, from terms like 'mise en place' and 'concierge' to nuanced phrases like 'service recovery' and 'comping a dish.' This ensures learners can communicate with precision.

Assessment-Focused: Each lesson ends with a 9-question quiz to measure understanding and retention, allowing learners to identify gaps and return to specific content as needed.

Our Global Community

Hospitality English Academy has touched the careers of professionals across six continents. From a restaurant server in São Paulo to a front desk agent in Dubai, our community is united by a pursuit of service excellence and a desire to communicate more effectively in English.

Through our courses, learners have been promoted to supervisory roles, secured positions at five-star hotels, and received recognition from their management teams for improved guest interaction. Every completed lesson represents a step toward a more confident, capable, and rewarding career in hospitality.

We foster a learning environment where curiosity is encouraged and the pursuit of excellence is celebrated. Our community of graduates continues to grow, forming a global network of bilingual hospitality professionals who support and inspire each other.

Future Vision

Looking ahead, Hospitality English Academy envisions a world where every restaurant and hotel professional, regardless of their mother tongue, can deliver a guest experience that is indistinguishable from the finest in the world. We are committed to:

  • Expanding our curriculum to include specialized tracks for fine dining, luxury hotels, and cruise ship hospitality
  • Developing AI-powered role-play simulations that allow learners to practice interactions in a virtual environment
  • Building certification partnerships with major hotel brands and restaurant groups for verified professional credentials
  • Measuring and publishing our educational impact on learners' careers and satisfaction scores
  • Continuing to innovate to remain the world's most practical and trusted hospitality English resource
