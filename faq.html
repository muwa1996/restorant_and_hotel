Frequently Asked Questions — Hospitality English Academy

Everything You Need to Know About Our Programme

This comprehensive FAQ section has been built in response to the questions our learners ask most frequently. We believe that transparency about how our courses work, what you can expect to achieve, and how our content is developed is itself a form of respect for your time and your professional ambitions. Read through these answers carefully — and if your specific question is not addressed here, please visit our Support page where our team is delighted to assist you directly.

The questions below are organized into four categories: Course Content and Structure; Learning Outcomes and Career Impact; Technical Access and Navigation; and Pricing and Enrollment. Each answer is written with the same commitment to clarity and professionalism that we ask of our learners in their own daily service communications.

Course Content and Structure

Q: How many lessons are in the programme and how long does each take?

The Hospitality English Academy curriculum comprises 20 comprehensive lessons, each covering a distinct topic area within the restaurant and hotel service spectrum. Each lesson is designed to be completed in approximately 45 to 90 minutes, depending on how carefully you engage with the reading materials and how many times you review the dialogue sections. The 9-question quiz at the end of each lesson typically takes an additional 10 to 15 minutes. The total programme, studied at a pace of one lesson per day, can be completed in 20 days. Studied more gently at three lessons per week, the full course spans approximately six to seven weeks.

Q: What topics does the course cover?

The curriculum covers the full spectrum of professional hospitality communication, from the foundational interactions of making reservations and checking in, through the nuanced skills of menu description, wine recommendation, complaint handling, and cultural sensitivity, to the advanced competencies of event management, upselling, and career interview preparation. Each lesson is written from the perspective of what a working hospitality professional actually needs to communicate effectively and confidently in their daily role. The content is empirically grounded in real-world hospitality scenarios and validated against international service standards.

Q: Is the content suitable for complete beginners in English?

Our programme is designed for learners with an intermediate level of English (approximately B1 to C1 on the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages). We assume familiarity with basic English grammar and a working vocabulary of everyday English, and build from that foundation into industry-specific professional English. Learners at a very early-stage English level (A1 or A2) would benefit from completing a general English foundation course before beginning this programme, as the professional vocabulary and extended reading texts require a degree of baseline fluency to engage with productively.

Learning Outcomes and Career Impact

Q: What specific improvements can I expect in my English after completing the programme?

Learners who complete all 20 lessons and engage fully with the dialogue and vocabulary sections consistently report three categories of improvement. First, vocabulary expansion: an additional 200 to 400 industry-specific terms and phrases across all areas of restaurant and hotel service, enabling more precise and professional communication. Second, interactive confidence: significantly greater comfort in guest-facing interactions, particularly in challenging scenarios such as complaint handling, upselling, and cultural differences. Third, structural fluency: the ability to organize service communications — recommendations, explanations, apologies, and farewells — into coherent, appropriate, and professional sequences rather than ad hoc responses.

Q: Will this course help me get promoted?

Promotion in hospitality is driven by a combination of factors: reliability, attitude, technical skill, team contribution, and — increasingly — communication quality. Our programme addresses the communication dimension directly and comprehensively. While we cannot promise promotion (which depends on factors entirely outside our influence), we can tell you that the learners in our programme who have advanced to management consistently identify improved English communication as a significant contributor to their visibility, credibility, and readiness in the eyes of their senior leadership. The programme is especially valuable to non-native English speakers working in English-speaking environments, where communication quality directly determines access to senior roles.

Technical Access and Navigation

Q: Do I need to create an account to access the lessons?

Our lessons are currently available without account creation, enabling immediate access from any device with internet connectivity. This open-access model reflects our belief that professional English education should be as accessible as possible to hospitality workers worldwide, many of whom may not have stable email accounts or consistent internet connectivity. Lessons can be bookmarked using your browser's built-in functionality to track progress between sessions.

Q: Can I do the lessons on my phone?

All lessons are fully optimized for mobile access. The responsive design adapts to smartphone and tablet screen sizes without any loss of functionality, enabling you to study during commutes, between service periods, or any time you have a few minutes available. The quiz functionality, video embed, and vocabulary grid are all fully operational on mobile devices.

Vocabulary: Talking About Learning in English

CurriculumThe full structured body of content offered in a course or educational programme, organized sequentially from foundational to advanced learning.
Self-pacedA mode of learning in which the learner controls the speed of progress, completing content on their own timeline rather than following a fixed class schedule.
RetentionThe ability to remember and recall information learned. High retention is achieved through repetition, application, and meaningful context — all of which our programme is designed to provide.
CompetencyA specific, demonstrably achievable professional ability. Our programme develops defined hospitality communication competencies that can be directly observed and evaluated in service.

About Hospitality Academy

Hospitality English Academy is dedicated to training restaurant and hotel professionals in the art of exceptional English communication. We provide comprehensive courses aligned with international service standards from Michelin-starred restaurants to five-star hotel brands.

Our Mission

To empower hospitality professionals worldwide with the linguistic confidence and industry-specific vocabulary needed to deliver a flawless guest experience, from the first greeting to the final farewell, in any international environment.

Educational Approach

Our interactive lessons combine authentic industry dialogues, rich vocabulary grids, and practical quizzes. Content is designed by senior hospitality professionals to ensure real-world accuracy and immediate applicability on the job.

Industry Impact

By training hospitality staff in professional English, we contribute directly to higher guest satisfaction scores, better online reviews, and greater career opportunities for our learners in the global hospitality industry.

Future Vision

We envision a world where every restaurant server and hotel professional can communicate with equal grace and clarity in English, regardless of their native language. Through innovative online education, we are building that future one lesson at a time.
